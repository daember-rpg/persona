Gallery — portraits come first: big full-bleed tiles with the name over the art and the faction's colour along the bottom; the card opens as a wide portrait on one side and the text on the other. Best when the art is good.Refs: Baldur's Gate 3 portraits, Persona 5 / Metaphor confidant cards, Dragon Age codex art.

The handout moment — when the GM shows someone for the first time, the scene dims, a glass band opens on a gold thread, the portrait slides in with a light sweep and the name writes itself. About 2 seconds, click to skip; it then becomes the card (or "Later" keeps it in the gallery). Smaller news — a story or secret learned later — gets a quiet pill at the top instead.Refs: Persona 5 / Metaphor character introductions, Pentiment's name cards, Sekiro / Elden Ring boss title cards.
