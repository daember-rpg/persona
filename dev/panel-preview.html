Captain Ilsa Marrow

Captain of the Embermark guard

EmbermarkCity Watch

Twenty years on the walls and never once late to a watch. Ilsa keeps the gate ledger herself and trusts no clerk with it.

She owes Lord Venn her post, and everyone at the Copper Kettle knows she hates it.

Secret uncovered

She lets one cart through the north gate every new moon, unchecked.
